PAINT PROTECTION FILM

Self-healing film to prevent your paint from rock chips, gravel spray, and road grime, keeping your ride looking pristine. Invisible armor for daily drivers and weekend trophies alike.

What PPF Does

PPF is, typically, a thermoplastic polyurethane film that is applied over your paint. PPF comes in clear to help protect your factory paint, or colored to give your ride an updated custom look while also protecting your factory paint. It is a self-healing film designed to absorb the hits that would otherwise leave chips and scratches, and it blocks UV rays to help eliminate paint fade or oxidization.

On Northwest roads that matters more than most places. Nine months of wet pavement means constant gravel spray off the car in front of you, and a front end with no film shows it inside a season.

Coverage Options

Front clip — bumper, partial hood, partial fenders, and mirrors. The highest-impact zone and the most common starting point.

Full front — full hood, full fenders, bumper, and mirrors, so there's no visible film line halfway up the hood.

Full vehicle — every painted panel covered. The complete answer for a car you intend to keep pristine.

What's Included

  • Full decontamination, clay, and prep before install
  • Computer-cut patterns plus hand-trimming where it matters
  • Wrapped edges where the panel allows, for an invisible line
  • Self-healing top coat and UV blocking on every film we carry
  • 1-year installation warranty on every job
