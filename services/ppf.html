PAINT PROTECTION FILM

Self-healing urethane film that takes the rock chips, gravel spray, and road grime so your paint doesn't. Invisible armor for daily drivers and weekend trophies alike.

What PPF Does

PPF is a clear urethane film applied over your paint. It absorbs the hits that would otherwise leave chips and scratches, and light swirls self-heal with heat — a warm day or a bucket of hot water and they're gone. It also blocks UV, so the paint underneath doesn't fade or oxidize.

On Northwest roads that matters more than most places. Nine months of wet pavement means constant gravel spray off the car in front of you, and a front end with no film shows it inside a season.

Coverage Options

Front clip — bumper, partial hood, partial fenders, and mirrors. The highest-impact zone and the most common starting point.

Full front — full hood, full fenders, bumper, and mirrors, so there's no visible film line halfway up the hood.

Full vehicle — every painted panel covered. The complete answer for a car you intend to keep pristine.

Track pack & high-wear — rocker panels, door cups, door edges, and the rear luggage area. Small pieces, big difference on resale.

Over a Wrap

PPF can go over a vinyl wrap as well as over paint. If you've committed to a color change and want it to survive the commute, this is how you do it — talk to us about layering when you book.
