CERAMIC COATING

A hydrophobic, gloss-boosting layer that bonds to the surface and stays there. Rain sheets off, grime struggles to stick, and a Tuesday downpour does most of the washing for you.

The Prep

A coating is only as good as the surface it cures onto — which is why most of the work happens before any coating comes out of the bottle. We start with a controlled hand wash, decontamination with iron remover and clay, and a full inspection under high-output lighting.

From there we correct. Swirls, wash marring, and light defects get polished out first, because a coating locks in whatever is underneath it. Skip this step and you've sealed the flaws in for years.

The Coating

Once the paint is right, the coating goes on panel by panel, levelled by hand, and then cures in a controlled environment before the car goes anywhere. The result is a slick, hydrophobic surface with real depth to the gloss — and a car that comes clean with far less effort every wash.

Over Paint, Wrap, or PPF

Ceramic isn't just for paint. Coating a vinyl wrap keeps the finish from staining and makes it far easier to wash, and coating PPF adds slickness on top of the film's impact protection. If you're doing more than one service, we'll sequence them properly.

What's Included

  • Two-bucket hand wash with pH-neutral shampoo
  • Iron decontamination and clay bar treatment
  • Paint correction to the level your package calls for
  • Panel-by-panel coating application and levelling
  • Controlled cure before handoff
  • Wheel faces, glass, and trim coated on request
  • Aftercare guidance and a maintenance wash schedule
