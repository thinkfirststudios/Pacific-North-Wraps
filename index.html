Why Pacific North Wraps

BUILT FOR NORTHWEST
ROADS.

Nine months of rain, road grime, and gravel spray is a brutal test for paint. Everything we install is chosen and applied with that in mind — edge-wrapped seams that won't lift in the wet, film that shrugs off chip damage, and coatings that make a Tuesday downpour do the washing for you.

Our bay is climate-controlled and dust-managed, because a wrap is only as good as the surface underneath it. Every job starts with a full decontamination and prep pass, and nothing gets rushed to make room for the next car.

Certified installers for 3M and Avery Dennison. Oregon-based, Pacific Northwest through and through — and we'd rather earn the next job than chase the quick one.
